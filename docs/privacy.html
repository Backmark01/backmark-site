Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Backmark is built to remember things for you, not learn things about you.

Current release

  • No account is required
  • Your app content is not stored on a Backmark server
  • No analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking
  • One App Store purchase; no subscription
  • Your Library and Boards stay on your iPhone unless you share or export them
  • No location permission; Photos access is add-only
  • The microphone is used only while you record a voice note
  • Siri and Search is off until you turn it on, and stays on this iPhone

Information stored by Backmark

Memories, Boards and plans (their dates, days and your notes), voice recordings and their written-down words, Board covers, recognized text, titles, descriptions, tags, topics, source links, places you add, map pictures, Library tiles and their icons, and attachments you choose to save are stored locally on your device in the app and its iOS Share Extension container. Your settings, including your Siri and Search choices and what you hid from it, are stored there too. If you enter an optional name or nickname, it is stored only on your iPhone and used for greetings.

A saved link only points to the original. If the original is deleted or made private, the link stops opening, but what Backmark saved stays on your iPhone and an export keeps a copy.

On-device processing

When you save a screenshot or image, Backmark uses Apple Vision on your device to recognize visible text so it can be searched. On compatible iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Backmark can use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models to suggest titles, summaries, and tags, to sort imported bookmarks into topics, in Ask this plan to answer questions using only the saves and your notes in a plan you choose, and, when Siya’s animations are on, to write a short comment about up to four saves you’re looking at. Ask never looks anything up online; questions about a trip’s days are read straight from your plan. A Board cover starts from a description Backmark writes on your iPhone from the Board’s own places and topics; covers and optional Library tile icons are created with Apple’s Image Playground from that description, which you can edit, and the image you keep is saved on your iPhone. Cook mode reads ingredients, steps and timers from a recipe’s own saved text, with no AI. When you import bookmarks, only each bookmark’s title, site, and folder name are read; the pages are never opened. You can turn the AI features off in Profile under On-device AI; saving, text recognition, voice notes, and search keep working. Backmark does not send your saved content to Backmark or to a cloud AI service.

Voice notes and the microphone

Backmark asks for the microphone the first time you tap Record, and uses it only while a recording you started is running, including with the screen locked. Your words are written down on your iPhone with Apple’s on-device speech recognition; Backmark never sends recordings to a server. iOS also asks for Speech Recognition permission, and Apple’s wording there can’t be changed by apps. If a language isn’t on your iPhone yet, iOS downloads it once from Apple, with visible progress. The recording and its words are stored on your iPhone and deleted with the voice note or with “Delete all local data.” Audio you share to Backmark from another app, such as Voice Memos, is written down the same way.

Siri and Spotlight

Siri and Search is available on iOS 18 or later and is off until you turn it on in Backmark. When it’s on, Backmark adds your saves’ titles, summaries, keywords, topics and places, and your plans’ names and purposes, to this iPhone’s own search index so Siri and Spotlight can find them. The full text of screenshots and voice recordings is not added, and neither are photos or files, although a save’s title, summary or keywords may have been written from that text (for example, a voice note is named after its first words). You can hide any plan, topic, or single save, and turning the switch off or deleting all local data removes everything Backmark added.

When you search Backmark or ask about a plan with Siri, Backmark finds the matching saves or writes the answer on your iPhone and gives the result to Siri to show or read out. Backmark itself sends nothing anywhere; what you say to Siri and what Siri shows you are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Apple Maps, link titles, Google short links, and Photos

Backmark uses these services only when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Place search (Apple Maps). When you search for a place, only the words you type are sent to Apple Maps. Your iPhone’s location is never sent, and Backmark does not ask for location permission.
  • Map links. Full Apple Maps and Google Maps links are read on your iPhone without being opened.
  • Google Maps short links. When you share a short maps.app.goo.gl link to Backmark or paste one into Add a place, Backmark asks Google only for that link’s first redirect, without loading a page, to find the place name. The name is then searched in Apple Maps.
  • Link titles. When you share a web link to Backmark, the Share sheet reads that page’s public title and description once to name the Memory. This request goes only to the website you shared, and nothing is sent to Backmark.
  • Apple Maps place IDs. Some Apple Maps links carry only an address and Apple’s own place ID. Backmark looks that place up in Apple Maps by its ID, sending only the ID, and otherwise searches the address.
  • Map pictures (Apple Maps). Map pictures for places are Apple Maps snapshots created for the place’s coordinates and stored on your iPhone. They are deleted with your local data.
  • Board routes (Apple Maps). A Board with two or more places shows a planned walking or driving route in the Board’s order. Only the stops’ coordinates are sent to Apple Maps to get directions. Backmark does not record or track where you go.
  • Photos (add-only). If you choose Download for a Board’s images, Backmark asks for add-only access to save them to Photos. Backmark cannot see your photo library.

Apple, Google and the websites you share process these requests under their own privacy policies. Backmark does not use your camera, and uses the microphone only as described under Voice notes.

Data sharing

We do not sell your information. Backmark shares content only when you explicitly use iOS sharing, share a plan as images, choose “Export all local data,” or open an original source. “Tell a friend” in Profile opens the iOS share sheet with a short message and Backmark’s App Store link; Backmark does not see whom you send it to. “Rate on the App Store” opens Apple’s App Store review page. The destination app or website then processes that information under its own privacy policy. “Report a bug” opens an email draft and does not attach your saved Backmark data.

Export and restore

“Export all local data” in Profile creates one zip file on your iPhone containing a readable Backmark.html page, a backmark-data.json file, and folders with your photos, files, voice recordings, Board covers, map pictures, and tile icons. You choose where it goes with the iOS share sheet. To bring an export back, choose + Add, then “Restore a Backmark export.” Restoring adds only what is missing and never deletes or overwrites what is already in Backmark.

Diagnostics and contact

Backmark does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK. If you email support, Google processes the email and any information you choose to include.

App Store purchase

Backmark is sold as a one-time paid app with no recurring subscription. Apple processes the App Store purchase and related payment information. Backmark does not receive your full card or bank details and does not include an in-app payment system.

Retention and deletion

Your local information remains until you delete individual Memories or Boards, choose “Delete all local data” (which also removes previews, attachments, voice recordings, covers, map pictures, tile icons, and everything Backmark added to Siri and Spotlight), or remove the app. iOS backups may retain app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Support website

These policy and support pages are hosted with GitHub Pages. Visiting them may provide GitHub with ordinary web request information under GitHub’s own privacy statement. This is separate from using the Backmark iPhone app.

Children

Backmark is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as Backmark changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
