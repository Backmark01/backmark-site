Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

Backmark is built to remember things for you—not learn things about you.

Current release

  • No account is required
  • Your app content is not stored on a Backmark server
  • No analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking
  • One App Store purchase; no subscription
  • Your Library and Board stay on your iPhone unless you share them

Information stored by Backmark

Memories, Boards, recognized text, titles, descriptions, tags, source links, and attachments you choose to save are stored locally on your device in the app and its iOS Share Extension container.

On-device processing

When you save a screenshot or image, Backmark may use Apple Vision to recognize visible text and, on compatible iPhones, Apple Foundation Models to organize it. This processing happens on your device. The public release does not send your saved content to Backmark or a third-party AI service.

Data sharing

We do not sell your information. Backmark shares content only when you explicitly use iOS sharing or open an original source. The destination app or website then processes that information under its own privacy policy.

Diagnostics and contact

Backmark does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK. If you email support, Google processes the email and any information you choose to include.

App Store purchase

Backmark is sold as a one-time paid app with no recurring subscription. Apple processes the App Store purchase and related payment information. Backmark does not receive your full card or bank details and does not include an in-app payment system.

Retention and deletion

Your local information remains until you delete individual Memories, choose “Delete all local data,” or remove the app. iOS backups may retain app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Support website

These policy and support pages are hosted with GitHub Pages. Visiting them may provide GitHub with ordinary web request information under GitHub’s own privacy statement. This is separate from using the Backmark iPhone app.

Children

Backmark is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as Backmark changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Backmark Support page.
